1. Our services

SLC Premier provides residential and commercial cleaning services in Salt Lake City, Utah and surrounding areas, including standard, deep, recurring, move-in / move-out, commercial, post-construction, and Airbnb / short-term rental turnover cleaning, plus optional add-on services. The tasks included in each service are described on our website and in your booking confirmation. Any task not listed is not included unless we agree to it in writing.

2. Booking and scheduling

  • Services can be booked through our online booking tool, by email, or by phone. A booking is confirmed once you receive a confirmation from us.
  • We provide an arrival window rather than an exact arrival time. Traffic, weather, or a previous job running long can occasionally affect arrival; we will let you know if we expect to be late.
  • Please provide accurate information when booking, including the size of the property, its condition, any pets, and any special instructions. Inaccurate information can affect pricing and the time needed (see Section 3).

3. Pricing and quotes

  • Prices shown on our website are starting prices. Your final price depends on the size and condition of your space, the service selected, any add-ons, and how often you book.
  • Quotes are based on the information you provide. If on arrival the property is significantly larger, dirtier, or more cluttered than described, or the job requires work outside the booked service, we will talk with you before continuing. We may adjust the price, limit the scope of work to the time booked, or reschedule.
  • Excessively dirty conditions, such as heavy buildup, pet waste, mold, or hoarding situations, may require additional charges or a separate quote.

4. Payment

  • We accept cash and major credit cards. Card details collected through our online booking tool are handled by our secure payment provider; we do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Unless otherwise agreed, payment is due on the day of service. When you book online, your card on file may be charged once the service is completed.
  • Gratuities are appreciated but never required.
  • Unpaid balances may be subject to a late fee, and we may pause future services until the account is brought current.

5. Cancellations, rescheduling and lockouts

  • We understand plans change. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a cleaning.
  • Cancellations or reschedules made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a late cancellation fee to cover the time we reserved for you.
  • If our team cannot access the property at the scheduled time (for example, no one is home, the key or code does not work, or the property is not ready), this is treated as a late cancellation and the same fee may apply.
  • We may need to reschedule due to severe weather, illness, or other circumstances outside our control. If that happens, we will contact you as early as possible and you will not be charged for the missed appointment.

6. Recurring service plans

  • Recurring plans (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) are billed per visit at your plan rate. Recurring rates apply as long as you keep your regular schedule.
  • There is no long-term contract. You can pause, change, or cancel a recurring plan at any time by contacting us before your next scheduled visit, in line with Section 5.
  • If visits are skipped or the schedule changes so that the home needs more work between cleanings, we may adjust your rate or recommend a deep clean to reset the space.

7. Access, safety and working conditions

  • You agree to provide safe access to the property, along with working water and electricity. If you are not home, please arrange a key, lockbox, or entry code in advance.
  • Please secure or put away valuables, cash, jewelry, firearms, prescription medication, and irreplaceable or fragile items before your cleaning.
  • Pets should be secured or kept in a separate area if they may be anxious, aggressive, or likely to leave the home when doors are opened. Please let us know about any pets when booking.
  • Our team may decline to clean any area they believe is unsafe or unsanitary, including areas with biohazards, bodily fluids, pests, mold, or hazardous materials.
  • We do not tolerate harassment of our staff. We may end a service immediately, and charge for time already worked, if our team is treated in an abusive or unsafe way.

8. Satisfaction guarantee

We stand behind our work. If something covered by your booked service was missed or not done to your satisfaction, please contact us within 24 hours of the cleaning and, where possible, include photos. We will come back to re-clean the areas in question at no additional cost. Re-cleans must be requested within this window and cover only the items included in the original booked service. Our guarantee is a re-clean; it is not a cash refund, except where required by law or at our discretion.

  • We are not responsible for damage that results from following your specific instructions, or from cleaning products or equipment you asked us to use.
  • We are not responsible for loss of, or damage to, cash, jewelry, collectibles, heirlooms, or items of high or sentimental value that were not put away before the service.
  • To the fullest extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim relating to a service is limited to the amount you paid for that service, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

10. Exclusions

Unless specifically agreed in writing, our services do not include:

  • Moving heavy furniture or appliances, or lifting items over a safe weight
  • Work that requires climbing above a standard step stool, including high ceilings, exterior windows, and gutters

  • Carpet shampooing, upholstery cleaning, or restoration of stained, etched, or damaged surfaces
  • Items that could be damaged by normal cleaning, such as antiques, artwork, or delicate fixtures
